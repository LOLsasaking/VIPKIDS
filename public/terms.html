VIP KIDS TRANSPORTATION

Terms of Service

Draft effective August 1, 2026

These terms govern use of the VIP Kids Transportation app. A signed transportation agreement separately controls pricing, schedules, cancellations, insurance, and service-specific obligations.

Service

The service coordinates prearranged transportation between approved families and assigned drivers. Parents and children cannot request an on-demand ride or select another driver.

Accounts and guardian consent

You must provide accurate information and protect your sign-in. A parent creating a child record or child login confirms they are the child’s parent or authorized guardian and consent to processing needed for transportation and safety.

Location and notifications

During an active route, a driver device shares precise location with assigned families and authorized operations staff. Positions and notifications may be delayed by connectivity, device settings, or platform delivery and are not the only emergency system.

Safety and emergencies

Drivers must complete required checks and follow applicable law and operating procedures. In an emergency, call 911 or the appropriate local emergency service first. In-app SOS and messaging supplement emergency services.

Acceptable use

Do not impersonate another person, access another family’s data, interfere with tracking, submit false events, misuse emergency tools, or bypass approval and security controls.

Availability and termination

Service may be interrupted for maintenance, connectivity, weather, school changes, or safety concerns. VIP Kids may suspend access for safety, fraud, noncompliance, or violation of these terms. Some records may be retained where legally required.
