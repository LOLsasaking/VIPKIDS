ACCOUNT & DATA

Delete your account

You can permanently delete your VIP Kids Transportation account and the data controlled by that account. Deletion is irreversible.

Option 1 — delete it yourself in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the VIP Kids Transportation app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Enter your password and type DELETE to confirm.

Your account is removed immediately when you confirm.

Option 2 — request deletion by email

If you cannot sign in — for example your account is pending approval or suspended — request deletion by email from the address on your account. Include the account email and your role (parent, driver, or child).

Request account deletion

Or email privacy@vipkidstransportation.com. We confirm the request with the account owner and complete verified deletions within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Parent accountThe account, assigned child transportation records, messages, schedules, and route history.
Driver accountThe account and driver location history; the driver is unassigned from active records.
Child sign-inOnly that restricted child login. The transportation record remains under parent and administrator control.

What may be retained

A limited set of records may be retained only where required by law or for safety, accident, and compliance obligations — for example transportation and incident records. Access to retained records is restricted to that purpose, and they are deleted at the end of the required retention period.
