VIP KIDS TRANSPORTATION

Support

Help with sign-in, assignments, routes, notifications, account deletion, and transportation service questions.

Contact support

Email gonxander@gmail.com. Include your account email and role, but never send your password, child medical information, or precise location by email.

Account approval

New parent and driver accounts remain pending until reviewed. Parents become active after an administrator assigns their child’s dedicated driver and vehicle.

Location or notification problems

Confirm location and notification permissions are enabled, battery-saving restrictions are disabled for active driver routes, and the phone has internet service. Never rely on the app as the only emergency method.

Emergency

For immediate danger or a medical emergency, call 911 or the applicable local emergency service first. Then contact VIP Kids operations using the number in your approved service agreement.
